## About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Deep Learning Theory Team at RIKEN AIP](https://www.riken.jp/en/research/labs/aip/generic_tech/deep_learn_theory/index.html). I received my Ph.D. in Economics from The University of Tokyo in 2025, where my dissertation focused on nonparametric Bayesian statistics for high-dimensional data.

My research lies at the intersection of statistics and machine learning theory. I am interested in understanding *why* and *when* modern, often over-parameterized, models generalize, and in designing principled Bayesian methods that scale to large and structured data.

## Research Interests

- Bayesian statistics and nonparametric Bayes
- Spatio-temporal modelling
- Statistical learning theory for large-scale models
- In-context learning and meta-learning theory
